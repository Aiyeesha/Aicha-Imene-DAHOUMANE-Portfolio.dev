## Context

Digit Learning, an online professional training school, had been using Salesforce for about 2 years with dedicated business objects (Student, Mentor, Training). The project manager commissioned an **internal quality audit** followed by a modernization plan.

This isn't a greenfield project: it's the most common real-life case on a Salesforce team — **improving an existing org without breaking it**. The audit, the right structural fix, the data migration, and measuring the gain are the heart of the work here.

## The quality audit

Method: interviews with key users — sales reps **and** management, to cross-check perspectives rather than starting from a single voice.

| Finding | Flagged by | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| **The data model only links a student to a single training** | Sales + management | Central finding: sales and pedagogical tracking impossible for multiple enrollments |
| Enrollment + mentor assignment > 30 min/student, no automation | Sales reps | Sales time consumed by data entry |
| No structured former-client tracking | Management | No loyalty lever, no way to measure satisfaction |
| Insufficient reports and metrics | Sales + management | Decisions made without reliable visibility |

## The structural fix: the junction object

The fix isn't a workaround — it's the **right model**. A new **Purchased Training** object in **master-detail to Student and to Training** turns the relationship into a proper many-to-many:

- carries its own attributes: start/end dates, type, price;
- permanently lifts the "one training per student" limit and enables multiple enrollments;
- unlocks **roll-up summaries** (available seats / maximum headcount on Training) — remaining capacity becomes a computed value, not a hand-maintained field.

Existing objects are enriched accordingly: Student (training history, *active-client* / *former-client* status), Mentor (availability), Training (available seats, maximum headcount).

## Automation

- **Enrollment + mentor assignment automated** — a direct audit recommendation, implemented to give sales reps back the time data entry was costing them.
- **Structured former-client tracking** + a process for **periodic review of training relevance**: the catalog no longer drifts for lack of being reviewed.

## The 3 reports delivered

| Report | For whom | Decision it supports |
|---|---|---|
| Trainings with available seats | Sales reps | Which session to offer, in real time |
| Students by status and mentor | Sales reps | Portfolio tracking |
| Prospect → active client conversion rate | Management | Commercial performance tracking |

## Deployment & migration

- **Deployment** of the app via Workbench (zip package, *Migration > Deploy*).
- **Migration of existing data** (students, mentors, trainings) via Data Loader, in dependency order — the risky part of any org change: carrying over the existing data with no loss or duplicates.

## Measured impact

| Process | Before | After | Measurement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mentor assignment + enrollment | > 30 min / student | 5 min / student | Per-student timing, before/after |
| Former-client tracking | > 15 min / contact | 10 min / contact | Per-contact timing |
| Catalog management & review | > 30 min / review | 15 min / review | Per-review timing |

The impact analysis is **qualitative and quantitative**: time saved is quantified per process, not in aggregate — which is what makes it defensible.

## Decisions & assumed limitations

| Decision | Alternative rejected | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Master-detail junction object** | Multiple lookup fields on Student | A clean many-to-many, with per-enrollment attributes and roll-up summaries; multiple lookups would have hit a ceiling and broken computed capacity |
| **Roll-up summary** for available seats | A counter maintained by automation | A platform-computed value doesn't drift out of sync |
| **Data Loader migration in dependency order** | Object-by-object import with no sequencing | Master-detail relationships require the parent to exist before the child |

Assumed limitations:

- **Master-detail cascades deletes**: deleting a Student deletes their Purchased Trainings. Acceptable here (an enrollment with no student is meaningless), but it's a constraint to know up front, not a side effect discovered later.
- **Gains measured on Dev Org datasets**, not on Digit Learning's full production volume — the order of magnitude is solid, the exact figure would need confirming at scale.
- **Time saved per process, not team-wide throughput**: the measurement covers an isolated task, not the full commercial pipeline.
